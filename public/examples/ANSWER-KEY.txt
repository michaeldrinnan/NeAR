NeAR example files
==================

Synthetic sustained "ah" vowels with increasing hoarseness (pitch jitter,
loudness shimmer and breath noise). All files have the same loudness.

TestItems  - 8 samples to rate (sample-A ... sample-H)
RefItems   - 5 references, ref-1 (clearest) ... ref-5 (roughest)

Answer key, clearest (best) first:
  sample-E, sample-B, sample-G, sample-A, sample-H, sample-D, sample-F, sample-C

With the references, the expected order is:
  ref-1, sample-E, sample-B, ref-2, sample-G, sample-A, ref-3, sample-H, sample-D, ref-4, sample-F, sample-C, ref-5

Generated by scripts/make-examples.mjs in https://github.com/michaeldrinnan/NeAR (MIT licence).
